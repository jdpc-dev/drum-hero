<p>Drum Hero is a mobile rhythm game similar to Rock Band, Guitar Hero, Clone Hero and YARG focused exclusively on the drum instrument.</p>
<p>Features:</p>
<ul>
<li>8-lane drum highway (Hi-Hat Closed/Open, Crash, Snare, Kick, Tom 1, Tom 2, Tom 3, Ride)</li>
<li>Three control modes: Default, Haptic (kick via accelerometer), and Custom lane reordering</li>
<li>Compatible with Clone Hero/YARG song format (MIDI charts + audio stems)</li>
<li>Load custom songs from external storage</li>
<li>Song timeline, combo counter, hit windows, shockwave effects</li>
<li>Free Mode for drum testing without a song</li>
</ul>
<p>Songs are loaded from the DrumHero directory in your storage.</p>
