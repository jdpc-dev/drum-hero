Drum Hero is a mobile rhythm game similar to Rock Band, Guitar Hero, Clone Hero and YARG focused exclusively on the drum instrument.

Features:
- 8-lane drum highway (Hi-Hat Closed/Open, Crash, Snare, Kick, Tom 1, Tom 2, Tom 3, Ride)
- Three control modes: Default, Haptic (kick via accelerometer), and Custom lane reordering
- Compatible with Clone Hero/YARG song format (MIDI charts + audio stems)
- Load custom songs from external storage
- Song timeline, combo counter, hit windows, shockwave effects
- Free Mode for drum testing without a song

Songs are loaded from the DrumHero directory in your storage.
